Single shared helper `_collect_ticket_files` (find -L + LC_ALL=C sort -z, NUL-delimited) replaces 9 non-recursive `$TICKETS_DIR/*.md` globs. Identity stays the frontmatter `id`, so no data-model change. Hidden directories are pruned; hidden .md files are still tickets. Deterministic alphabetical ordering preserved. Fixed two latent bugs: listing commands exited 2 on an empty tickets tree, and `closed` word-split paths with spaces. 37 new BDD scenarios (168 total, green), including mutation-proven stdin-hang guards.
